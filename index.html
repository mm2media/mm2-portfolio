Concept & script

Story, boards and a shot list written for what AI does well — and where it needs help.

STEP 2

Generate

Structured prompting and reference control for consistent characters, products and look.

STEP 3

Craft

ABOUT

Marlon Muñoz is a creative director and visual technologist working where design craft meets AI.

For 25 years he has designed in 3D and motion — illustration, logos, websites, AR/VR and projection mapping — for brands including Meta, Adobe, Coca-Cola, Intel and Michelin. Today MM2 Media puts that experience behind AI-driven commercials and the custom workflows that make them repeatable.
